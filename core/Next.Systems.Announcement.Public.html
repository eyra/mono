    Next.Systems.Announcement.Public (mica v0.1.0)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    Create, change and read announcements and their recipients.

    A recipient row (Next.Systems.Announcement.RecipientModel) records that one user received an announcement and when they read it. Only users with a recipient row may open a published announcement (get_received/2).

    The filters that narrow the audience of an announcement. The audience is always creators; these come on top, combined with AND.

    count_audiences(announcements)

    create_announcement(user, attrs)

    Creates an announcement by author.

    deliver_announcement(announcement_id)

    Gives every recipient of a published announcement a recipient row and a To-do, with one insert for the rows and batched inserts for the To-dos. For a high-priority announcement it also queues one email per recipient (Next.Systems.Announcement.EmailTemplate), in batches. Recipients who already have a row are skipped, so running it again adds and sends nothing. Users deleted since publication are left out. Returns {:ok, count} with the number of recipients added.

    list_audience_ids(filters, query)

    The ids of the users an announcement for the audience of filters and query reaches: creators with a confirmed account that match every filter and every word of the search, with the matching of Admin → Users (Systems.Admin.Public.list_matching_users/2). Filters other than audience_filters/0 are ignored.

    list_received(user, opts \\ [])

    The published announcements user received, as recipient rows with the announcement preloaded, newest publication first. limit: n returns only the n newest.

    The recipient rows of announcement.

    mark_read(recipient)

    Marks the announcement read for the recipient and clears its To-do. read_at is set only once and keeps the time of the first opening. The To-do is cleared on every call, so one that is left or comes back after the first opening goes too. Returns the recipient with read_at set.

    publish_announcement(announcement, filters, query, shown_count)

    Publishes an unpublished announcement to the audience of filters and query (list_audience_ids/2). The audience is resolved here, once, and stored on the announcement; a job then gives every recipient a recipient row and a To-do (deliver_announcement/1).

    update_announcement(loaded, attrs)

    Changes an announcement and dispatches {:announcement, :updated}, so the pages that show it refresh. Nothing is sent again and the recipients keep their read status.

    publish_announcement(announcement, filters, query, shown_count)

    Publishes an unpublished announcement to the audience of filters and query (list_audience_ids/2). The audience is resolved here, once, and stored on the announcement; a job then gives every recipient a recipient row and a To-do (deliver_announcement/1).

    shown_count is the recipient count the admin saw and confirmed. When the audience has changed since, nothing is published and the result is {:error, {:audience_changed, count}} with the current count.

    Returns {:error, :no_recipients} when the audience is empty and {:error, :already_published} when it was published before.

    update_announcement(loaded, attrs)

    Changes an announcement and dispatches {:announcement, :updated}, so the pages that show it refresh. Nothing is sent again and the recipients keep their read status.

    announcement is the copy the change was made on, such as the one a form opened. Returns {:error, :changed_since_loaded} when the announcement was saved since that copy was loaded, so two forms can't undo each other's changes. After publication only the title, summary and body may change (Next.Systems.Announcement.Model.fixed_after_publishing/0). Returns {:error, :already_published} when the change touches another field, and when the copy was loaded before it was published. The row is locked and checked in the same transaction as the update.
