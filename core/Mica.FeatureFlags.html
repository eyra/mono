    Mica.FeatureFlags (mica v0.1.0)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    Feature flags. Systems declare the flags they read (Mica.System.feature_flags/0), the SaaS sets their defaults (Mica.SaaS.feature_flags/0), and the app config (config :mica, :features, and ENABLED_APP_FEATURES at runtime) has the final say.

    A declared flag is off unless the SaaS or the config turns it on; a flag that is neither declared nor configured is off. A configured name that no system declares stays in the effective list, with a warning at boot (warn_undeclared/0).
