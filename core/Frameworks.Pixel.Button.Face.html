    Frameworks.Pixel.Button.Face (mica v0.1.0)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    The look of a button, inside a Frameworks.Pixel.Button.Action. Every face but the menu faces renders Prism's button (Frameworks.Pixel.button/1) as a <div>, since the action is the clickable element.

    bg_color, text_color, border_color and font take Tailwind classes that override Prism's look; without them a face has its Prism variant's colors and typography.

    Prism's button is inline (inline-flex); the icon, label and *_icon faces take a line of their own (flex), so no line box adds height around them.
